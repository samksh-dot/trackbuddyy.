01. Where to go?

Curated Heritage Discovery

Curated architectural monuments, museums, royal havelis, and bustling bazaars with opening hours and entrance ticket rates.

02. Best visit order?

Route & Stop Optimization

Nearest-neighbor geometric sequencing avoids crisscrossing city traffic, cutting travel fatigue and keeping group energy high.

03. How to travel?

Transport Comparison

Transparent fare tables comparing Walking, Auto-Rickshaw, App Cabs (Uber/Ola), Metro, and Local Buses for your exact group size.

04. What will it cost?

Deterministic Expense Engine

Exact arithmetic for stay, food, transit, and monument fees with verified student concessions (up to 75% off for university ID holders).

Vetted Hostels & Stays

Filter by rating, student discounts, and distance to attractions. Includes honest occupancy availability indicators.

06. What if plans change?

Disruption Replanning

Crowd-aware reordering and weather alerts adapt your daily schedule if rain strikes or a fort experiences peak morning congestion.
